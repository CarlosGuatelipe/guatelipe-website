## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Not a generic template wearing a print skin — the composition genuinely commits to its own conceit. The numbered rail-as-table-of-contents, the `work-index` table styled as a magazine masthead, crop-mark corners on project plates, "N.º 001 / 002 vaga aberta" framing, and the ghost numeral behind each project card only make sense for an editorial-portfolio metaphor, carried consistently from `<title>` to the footer colophon. The copy is specific to this person — "já sei que você vai perguntar o preço" is a real, personality-bearing line no template ships with. That said, the base layer (three animated blurred color blobs plus two blurred "fog" ellipses, `filter:blur(90–120px)`) directly contradicts the system's own stated invariant and is exactly the ambient-glow language DESIGN.md names as a forbidden world. The surface vocabulary is distinctive and well-executed; the atmosphere underneath is generic effects-library filler undermining the discipline the rest of the page is proud of.

**Deterministic scan:** `detect.mjs --json index.html` ran clean except one finding: `overused-font` (warning, slop category) at `index.html:93`, flagging the string "instrument serif". This is very likely a false positive — Instrument Serif is not one of the six faces the rule actually targets (Inter, Roboto, Fraunces, Geist, Plus Jakarta Sans, Space Grotesk); the match appears to be on the substring rather than the specific blocklist, and no listed face appears anywhere in the page.

**Browser evidence:** Screenshots were not obtainable this session (compositor unavailable in the browser pane), so evidence comes from computed styles, network log, and DOM inspection on the live site. Confirmed independently of Assessment A: `--ink-mute` (#8A8271) on `--paper` (#FBFAF7) computes to ≈3.65:1, below WCAG AA's 4.5:1 for normal text. Confirmed: accent (#C2371C) on paper passes AA at ≈5.2–5.45:1. Confirmed: `--ink-soft` and body ink both pass comfortably (8.25:1 and 17.9:1). ## Overall Impression

The page is more specific and more disciplined than the median freelancer portfolio — the editorial/print-proof metaphor is carried all the way through interaction design, not just visuals, and the copy has a real human voice. The single biggest opportunity is closing the gap between what the system says it is and what it does: DESIGN.md declares "sem gradiente, sem brilho, sem vidro" as invariant while the backdrop actively runs a blurred animated gradient on every page load, and the FAQ still instructs users to use a contact form that was deliberately removed. Both are leftover contradictions, not fresh design problems, which makes them fast to fix and worth fixing first.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Dead "formulário" reference in the FAQ section, twice**
- **Why it matters:** `index.html:742` and `:749` both instruct the user to use a contact form. No `<form>` exists anywhere in the page — PRODUCT.md documents its removal as deliberate ("Sem formulário de contato, por decisão"). This sits directly before the contact section, the highest-stakes moment in the funnel: a user reading the FAQ for reassurance before messaging a stranger about money is told to do something impossible.
- **Fix:** Replace both instances with WhatsApp/e-mail routing — e.g. "Se a sua não estiver aqui, é só chamar no WhatsApp — respondo pessoalmente" and "…me chama no WhatsApp ou e-mail e envio uma proposta sob medida, sem compromisso."
- **Suggested command:** $impeccable clarify

**[P1] Animated blurred gradient "wash/fog" backdrop violates the design system's own stated invariant**
- **Why it matters:** `.backdrop-wash` (×4) and `.fog` (×2) in `css/style.css` (~139–219) use `radial-gradient(...)` with `filter:blur(90–120px)`, animating continuously (34–74s loops) behind the whole page. DESIGN.md states "Don't usar gradiente, brilho ou vidro... é invariante da implementação," and the CSS file's own header comment repeats it. This is the exact "brilho, partículas" language the system explicitly rejects, in a lighter palette, and it's the largest deviation from "a densidade é editorial, não de aplicativo."
- **Fix:** Remove the wash/fog layer (the paper-grain texture alone likely carries the "page that breathes" intent), or replace with a static/low-amplitude solid wash with zero blur, consistent with "deslocamento sólido, sem desfoque" used elsewhere.
- **Suggested command:** $impeccable polish

**[P1] `--ink-mute` fails WCAG AA contrast at the sizes it's actually used for**
- **Why it matters:** `--ink-mute:#8A8271` on `--paper:#FBFAF7` computes to ≈3.65:1 (confirmed independently by both assessments), applied at 9–11px monospace to `.colophon dt`, `.work-index td:first-child` (project numbers), `.contato-texto small`, menu icon glyphs, and multiple metadata lines. This is the token DESIGN.md designates for "texto de apoio e metadados" — used pervasively, not incidentally — and it lands on exactly the content carrying credibility signal (project N.º, year, client name, "No ar" status).
- **Fix:** Darken `--ink-mute` toward `--ink-soft` (#544C40, 8.25:1) for text under ~14px, or reserve `--ink-mute` for elements ≥18px/bold where 3:1 suffices.
- **Suggested command:** $impeccable audit

**[P2] Standards section ties the 48h response promise to e-mail while every other CTA promotes WhatsApp**
- **Why it matters:** `index.html:280–281` reads "Resposta ao orçamento / Em dias úteis, por e-mail," while the hero, the contact card, and PRODUCT.md's own "WhatsApp primeiro" principle all frame WhatsApp as primary and fastest. A scanning mobile user who only reads the stat tiles gets a channel signal that contradicts the actual funnel.
- **Fix:** Change "por e-mail" to "por WhatsApp," or drop the channel qualifier entirely.
- **Suggested command:** $impeccable clarify

**[P3] Toast micro-copy ("✨ E-mail copiado!") breaks the page's own single-accent rule**
- **Why it matters:** `js/script.js:302` uses a sparkle emoji in the copy-confirmation toast. DESIGN.md's "Regra do Acento Único" bans any second marker outside the single orange, and PRODUCT.md's voice guidance is "direta, sem jargão e sem promessa de vendedor" — a decorative emoji is a small lapse into generic-template flourish.
- **Fix:** Drop the emoji; let the monospace label and accent-colored toast border carry the "confirmed" signal.
- **Suggested command:** $impeccable polish
